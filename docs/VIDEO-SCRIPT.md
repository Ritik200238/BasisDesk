# BasisDesk — 2-minute demo video script

Screen-recording walkthrough. Target run time **1:55–2:00**. Written muted-first: the
**captions** carry the story on their own, because many judges watch with sound off the first
time. Voiceover (VO) is optional polish — your voice or an AI voice (ElevenLabs, calm/confident,
~150 wpm).

Everything shown is real and live at **basisdesk.vercel.app**. No claim here is unverifiable.

## The arc (why this order)

Hook → the live 11-market board (mainnet + breadth) → the SoSoValue brain (the sponsor moment) →
the neutrality proof (the wow) → deposit + risk receipt (completeness + non-custodial) → craft
montage (product, brand, tests) → Verify + CTA. Strongest visual first, strongest proof last.

## The proof beats (make sure each one lands on camera)

- **Mainnet is real** — the header pill + the `LIVE, MAINNET` labels + the stat strip. Say it, show it.
- **Market fit** — "the strategy that grew Ethena to billions." Proven demand, made accessible.
- **The SoSoValue brain** — the sponsor's data driving a real decision (de-risk), with a cited "why." Spend real time here.
- **The wow** — the flat hedge line vs the swinging hold line. This is the single most convincing 5 seconds.
- **Completeness** — deposit → receipt → non-custodial sign; methodology; brandkit; tests. A finished product.
- **Retention, honestly** — read-before-connect means zero-friction first value, and the de-risk signal is a reason to come back. (Show, don't claim numbers.)
- **Verifiability** — the Verify page as the closer. Confidence, not bragging.

## Production checklist (do this before you hit record)

1. **Warm the site first.** Load the home board, the BTC vault, methodology, brandkit, and verify once each, so data is cached and everything appears instantly on camera — **no spinners, no "rate limited."**
2. **Clean browser.** Hide the bookmarks bar and extensions; use a fresh window; light theme; 1920×1080 (or 1440×900 scaled to 1080p).
3. **Slow, deliberate cursor.** Let each screen settle ~1 second before you act. Smooth scrolls, no jitter.
4. **Record 30fps, keep total ≤ 2:00.** Trim dead air in edit.
5. **Burn in the captions** (Descript / CapCut). Put the keyword in the brand blue (`#1B4DFF`). Captions must make sense with sound off.
6. **VO + music.** Your voice or ElevenLabs, mid-pace and calm. Music low and minimal — it should never fight the VO.
7. **End card 3s:** BasisDesk mark · basisdesk.vercel.app · github.com/Ritik200238/BasisDesk.
8. **One takes-checklist:** BTC vault is showing live flow (warm it), the simulator starts at −30%, the deposit input reads a clean round number.
